Instructions

We need your help!

Our scientists have just discovered some new alien life forms on Mars. They've started organizing them into taxonomies but have become overwhelmed by the sheer number of aliens. That's where you come in! We have some alien taxonomies that our scientists have started, but many aliens still need to be classified into them. Your task is to place each alien into the most appropriate category. If no suitable category exists, you will be free to create a new one. Take note, small changes in appearance can mean big changes in behaviour, so make sure to pay attention and sort them as accurately as possible.

First you will do a tutorial to get familiar with the setup. The tutorial will involve categorizing animals. Click continue when you are ready to start.
